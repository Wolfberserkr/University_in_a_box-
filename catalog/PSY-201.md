# PSY-201 — The Evolved Mind

**Level:** 200 · **Credits:** 3 · **Weeks:** 14 · **Load:** ~5 h/week
**Prerequisite:** PSY-101 · **Unblocks:** PSY-301, PSY-302

---

## 1. Question

**Why does the machinery have this shape rather than another one?**

## 2. Why 200-level

Primary sources become the default. The student is expected to state a finding's magnitude and its boundary unprompted — PSY-101 taught that; here it's assumed. Weeks 5–6 are a primary-source-only block: the founding evolutionary-psychology statement and its most serious critic, read against each other with no secondary summary permitted.

## 4. Cut list

| Cut | Reason |
|---|---|
| Cellular and molecular neuroscience | Real, hard, unblocks no week in this major. |
| Primatology beyond what Sapolsky covers | One book's worth is the correct dose. |
| Group selection as a standalone dispute | Consumes three weeks, changes no downstream conclusion here. |
| Evolutionary psychology's culture-war reception | The scientific critique (wk 6) is stronger and more useful than the political one. |

## 5. Sources

Mostly `[R]` — **verify before buying.** Sapolsky and Henrich are the two acquisitions that matter; the rest can wait for the week that needs them.

**Obtainability:** Sapolsky and Henrich are trade books, widely available. Trivers and Hamilton are open. Buller may need a library.

## 6. Assessment

- **Midterm wk 7:** the Tutor probes whether you can state the evolutionary-psychology critique in its strongest form — a student who can only caricature Buller has not read week 6.
- **Term paper, 3,000 words:** *"Three behaviors I observe weekly at work, explained at four time scales."* First use of your own material as evidence. `ASSESSMENT.md` B2 binds: the examples cannot all come from the books.
