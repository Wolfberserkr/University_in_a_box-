# PSY-401 — Capstone: The Decision Audit

**Level:** 400 · **Credits:** 3 · **Weeks:** 8, intensive · **Load:** ~10 h/week
**Prerequisite:** PSY-202 **and** PSY-301 **and** PSY-302 · **Unblocks:** —

The prerequisite is strict and enforced. PSY-202 supplies the framework, PSY-301 the situational reading, PSY-302 the dispositional one. Attempting this course without all three produces an audit that measures the auditor.

---

## 1. Question

**Did human judgment, in these specific documented cases, fail in a direction a framework could have predicted?**

Answerable wrongly — which is why it qualifies as a capstone and "design a better review process" does not.

## 2. Why 400-level

Produces something that could be **wrong in public**. Graded by reconstruction rather than by argument: the framework either predicts recorded outcomes or it doesn't, and no quality of prose rescues it. This is the single course where the student's opinion of their own work carries no weight at all.

## 3. Sequence — 8 weeks

| Wk | Milestone | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 45 | Case selection against criteria fixed in advance. De-identification at intake | `C4`, `C5` |
| 46 | Coded index built and held separately. Cases that can't be de-identified are excluded, not softened | `C5` |
| 47–48 | **Blind scoring pass.** Decision-time information only, outcomes not consulted | `C2` |
| 49 | Outcome pass. Hit rate versus base rate, calibration by confidence bin, direction of error, Brier if probabilistic | `C3` |
| 50 | Write the analysis. **Expect the framework to have partly failed** | `C6` |
| 51 | Editor: three full rewrite rounds against the locked rubric | Part A |
| 52 | Final. Registrar closed. Year-two reading list written by you, not by an agent | Part D |

## 4. Cut list

| Cut | Reason |
|---|---|
| Adding cases after scoring begins | Sample discipline. `C4` fails on any post-hoc addition. |
| Improving the framework mid-audit | `C1` fails on any modification. The honest write-up of *why you wanted to* is worth more than a fabricated pass. |
| Additional reading during the capstone | Eight weeks of building. The reading was the previous forty-four. |
| Comparison against a published benchmark | Tempting, and it converts a clean audit into an unfalsifiable argument about comparability. |

## 5. Sources

None new. The framework was written in PSY-202 wk 14 and is already timestamped. The material is your own records.

**Hard constraints** (from `catalog/PSY-major.md` §1): de-identify at intake not write-up; nothing proprietary leaves your machine; the artifact is the analysis and the framework, never the underlying records. The **access check** was owed in Term B — if it wasn't done, this course does not open.

## 6. Assessment

Graded under `ASSESSMENT.md` Part C in full. Restated here because it's the thing most likely to be rationalised away at week 50:

> **A framework that performs at chance is not a failed capstone. A framework that performs at chance and is written up as a success is a failed capstone.**

Reporting a null result cleanly is the outcome PSY-101 exists to make possible. If you reach week 49 and the numbers are flat, you have completed the major correctly and the paper writes itself around that finding.
